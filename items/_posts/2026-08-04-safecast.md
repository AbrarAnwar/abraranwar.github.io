VLA policies fail under deployment-time shifts like clutter, lighting changes, and reworded instructions. SAFECAST uses contrast set perturbations to train and calibrate hidden-state risk probes, improving failure detection ROC-AUC over state-of-the-art baselines on both real-world DROID and LIBERO simulation.
