Robometer is a general-purpose, video-language-input dense reward model trained on RBM-1M, a dataset of over 1M trajectories spanning 21 robot embodiments. It improves robot learning across online RL, offline RL, model-based RL, failure detection, and data retrieval for imitation learning.
