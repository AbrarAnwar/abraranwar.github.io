DiPS is a Q-learning framework that dynamically selects persuasion strategies based on the evolving conversational context. In a fire-rescue evacuation scenario, DiPS achieves higher persuasion success than zero-shot LLM and RAG-augmented approaches in both simulated and real human interactions.
